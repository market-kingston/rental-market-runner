Kingston

Kingston is consistently ranked as one of the best cities to live in Canada. Its central location on Lake Ontario, between Toronto, Ottawa, and Montreal, makes it accessible while still offering the benefits of a smaller city. Known as the “Limestone City” for its historic architecture, Kingston combines heritage character with modern conveniences, making it an attractive place to call home.

The city’s rental market is shaped by its strong student population, anchored by Queen’s University, St. Lawrence College, and the Royal Military College. Each year, thousands of students create high demand for rentals close to campus and downtown. Apartments, shared houses, and student-oriented housing are all important parts of Kingston’s rental landscape.

At the same time, Kingston appeals to families, professionals, and retirees. Steady employment in healthcare, government, and education supports consistent demand across a range of neighbourhoods. From downtown apartments and heritage homes to suburban townhouses and larger rental properties, the city offers a wide mix of housing options.

Our flagship office is located in the heart of downtown Kingston, putting us at the centre of the community we serve. This allows us to provide local expertise, responsive service, and a strong connection to both residents and property owners across the city.

With its strong rental demand, reputation as a desirable place to live, and balance of affordability and accessibility, Kingston stands out as a reliable market for both students and long-term residents.
