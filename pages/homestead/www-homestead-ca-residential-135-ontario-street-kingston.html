Kingston

Carruthers Wharf Apartments

135 Ontario Street
1-844-966-1288

Welcome to Carruthers Wharf, Kingston's premier waterfront community at 135 Ontario Street. Experience the charm of old Kingston while enjoying the vibrant energy of the downtown waterfront. Our waterfront apartments offer easy access to downtown Kingston, Queen’s University and the Kingston Yacht Club. From your suite, take in panoramic views of Lake Ontario and the historic harbor.

Choose from thoughtfully designed one-bedroom with den, two-bedroom, and two-bedroom with den layouts, each equipped with modern appliances, including a fridge, stove, dishwasher, and microwave. Carruthers Wharf also offers a variety of amenities to enrich your lifestyle. Stay active in the fitness room, socialize in the party room, or host a summer gathering on the rooftop patio with BBQs, all while enjoying breathtaking waterfront views. On-site laundry facilities add even more convenience.

Discover the perfect blend of comfort, style, and prime location at Carruthers Wharf. Visit our rental office today to explore your new home and experience the exceptional lifestyle of Kingston’s waterfront living.
