More about us

Questions, answered.

Is tenant insurance required?

Yes. Tenant insurance is mandatory for all tenants, in accordance with the requirements of the Residential Tenancies Act. Queen's Off-Campus Living Advisor explains why it matters.

How do I report maintenance?

Report it within 24 hours on our maintenance and repairs page, with a description, the room and a photo if you can. We typically respond to and complete requests within 24 to 48 hours. For urgent problems like a burst pipe or no heat, call 613-531-4498 or 613-572-7276. For emergencies like a fire or break-in, call 911 first, then call us.

How do I lease a home?

Book a tour of the home you like. When your group is ready, everyone signs one Ontario Standard Lease together, a 12 month joint and several lease. A last month's rent deposit is collected when the lease is signed.

What does Pending mean?

Pending means that we don't know yet if this unit will be available for May 2027. We should find out soon. Sign up and pick the homes you like, and you'll hear from us first, as soon as they become available!

How do I see a place?

Open the listing and press Book a Tour. Tell us when works for you, and we'll get back to you within 24 hours to confirm.
