Queen's Student Housing since 2003

We were Queen's students too.

We're Queen's alumni, and have lived in this neighbourhood for decades. We know what makes a student rental great: a short walk to class, a place that's properly looked after, and a landlord who actually answers.

Decades of experience. Generations of Queen's students.

Since 2003, we've been housing Queen's students in the best locations around campus. Over those decades we've gained a deep understanding of the market, and that experience is why we are a recognized leader in off-campus student housing at Queen's today. We've been part of the Queen's experience for generations.

Owned and managed by us

We own every home we rent, and we manage it ourselves. When you rent from us, you're dealing with the people who own the house and who live in your community. We care about our tenants and we care about our buildings, because they're ours.

The best locations, on purpose

Every home was chosen for where it sits:

  • beside campus on Union and Collingwood;
  • in the heart of it all at University and Johnson;
  • close to groceries and bars around Princess and Division;
  • downtown on Brock and Sydenham, across from Hotel Dieu Hospital.

All of them are a short walk to Queen's, KGH, Hotel Dieu and downtown. We walked every route ourselves, so the walk times on our listings are real.

Looked after, properly

Tenants say it in review after review.

  • Our own maintenance crew, plus great relationships with a trusted network of local trades and suppliers.
  • 24/7 maintenance. When something breaks, we fix it properly, the first time.
  • Fast answers. Questions about your lease, your home or your move-in? We pick up the phone and answer emails.

Fair, and built for students

We work hard to keep our rents fair and cater to the unique needs of post-secondary students:

  • 12 month leases, most starting May 1;
  • the Ontario Standard Lease;
  • a move-in guide covering utilities, internet, garbage days and bus routes.

Tenant insurance is mandatory for all tenants, in accordance with the requirements of the Residential Tenancies Act.

Parking too

Reserved parking in our private open air lots near campus, with winter maintenance. See the parking lots.
