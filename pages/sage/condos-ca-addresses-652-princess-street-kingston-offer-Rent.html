    Description
    A unit with its own underground parking spot! Welcome to 326-652 Princess St., ideally located in the heart of Kingston's vibrant student community, just minutes from Queen's University. This 500 square foot, bright, clean, and furnished 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom unit features a spacious bedroom, a 4-piece bathroom, in-suite laundry, and a modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances. Furniture included with the unit consists of a double bed with mattress, side table, dining table with two chairs, coffee table, loveseat, media unit, and 50" flat-screen TV, making this a convenient turnkey opportunity. Residents enjoy excellent building amenities, including a fully equipped fitness centre, a party/meeting room, and a rooftop terrace with BBQs and scenic views. The unit monthly condo fees are $526.10. Conveniently located steps from public transportation, with quick access to Queen's University, St. Lawrence College, hospitals, downtown Kingston, shopping, dining, and everyday amenities. Both the building and unit are professionally managed, adding to the ease and convenience of ownership. With excellent rental potential, this turnkey unit is an ideal opportunity for students or investors!
